# BEFORE AND AFTER ANALYSIS

## Project Name
Cloud Computing Basics and Free-Tier Cost Awareness

## Before Using AI
- Beginners may find cloud computing terms difficult to understand.
- Comparing AWS, Azure, and Google Cloud services may take more time.
- Free-tier limits and billing risks may not be clear.
- Students may not know how to verify cloud pricing.

## After Using AI
- Cloud concepts can be explained in simple language.
- Service comparisons can be prepared more quickly.
- A safety checklist helps users remember important precautions.
- A quiz helps beginners test their understanding.
- Official documentation can be used to verify AI-generated information.

## Benefits
- Saves research time.
- Improves understanding of cloud concepts.
- Supports organized documentation.
- Encourages responsible use of AI tools.

## Limitations
- AI can provide incorrect or outdated information.
- Current pricing and free-tier conditions must be checked using official sources.
- AI-generated content requires human review.

## Conclusion
AI can support beginners in learning cloud computing more efficiently. However, users must verify important information and monitor cloud usage to avoid unexpected charges.
